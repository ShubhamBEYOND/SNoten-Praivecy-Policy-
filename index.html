Privacy Policy for SNoten

Effective Date: November 11, 2026 | Application: SNoten (com.aistudio.snoten.app)

Welcome to SNoten. Your privacy and data ownership are our absolute top priorities. SNoten is designed as an offline-first personal notes application.

1. Data Collection and Storage

SNoten operates entirely offline on your device. We do NOT collect, harvest, transmit, track, or store any of your personal data, notes, images, checklists, or reminders on external cloud servers.

  • Local Storage: All notes, categories, tags, images, and settings are stored locally on your device in a private SQLite Room database.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, create an account, or sign in to use SNoten.

2. Permissions Requested

SNoten requests only essential Android permissions required to deliver core functionality:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used solely to display local reminder alerts on your device when a note alarm triggers.
  • Alarm Scheduling (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used to schedule exact date and time note reminders locally.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Used for tactile feedback when reminders trigger.
  • Photo Picker: Uses the zero-permission Android System Photo Picker when you attach images to notes.

3. Security & PIN Lock

SNoten provides optional Master PIN security for protecting private notes. Security PIN keys are stored locally and encrypted using standard Android Keystore APIs.

4. Data Ownership & Export

You have 100% control over your data. SNoten includes full Backup & Restore features, allowing you to export your entire database as a JSON file or import backups whenever you choose.

5. Third-Party Services

SNoten does NOT integrate third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks that monitor user behavior.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app enhancements. Any changes will be updated directly within this page.
